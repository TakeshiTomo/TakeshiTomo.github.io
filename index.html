About

I am a robotics researcher working on tactile sensing, soft robotics, and robotic manipulation.

My research focuses on vision-based tactile sensors inspired by biological structures and their application to robotic manipulation.

Research

Vision-Based Tactile Sensing

Development of vision-based tactile sensors for measuring contact deformation and interaction between robots and objects.

Robot Manipulation

Robotic manipulation using tactile and visual information, including manipulation of thin objects.

Soft Robotics

Development of compliant robotic mechanisms and soft robotic hands capable of adaptive grasping.
